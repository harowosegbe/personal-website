With over six years in the 3D and immersive technology industries, Hammed has a strong track record of impactful contributions to virtual and augmented reality, including collaborations with leading brands like Marriott, Mastercard, Lloyds, and L'Oreal.

As Chapter President of the VR/AR Association in Nigeria, Hammed drives VR and AR adoption across Africa, advocating for innovation and inclusivity in these fields. He also pioneered the development of SwiftXR, a no-code platform empowering over 25,000 users to easily create and share 3D, augmented, and virtual reality experiences, making immersive content accessible to all.

### Key Technical Contributions
- **SwiftXR Development**: Launched a no-code platform that enables over 25,000 users to build 3D, AR, and VR experiences, transforming immersive content creation.
- **Smart Glasses for Real-Time Language Translation**: Engineered smart glasses with real-time AI translation, supporting Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa languages. This groundbreaking project enhances accessibility and cultural connectivity through on-the-spot language translation.
- **3D Art Tool**: Created a tool that transforms 2D images into interactive 3D artwork, now widely used by digital artists and designers.
- **AI Project with Lloyds Bank**: Developed a real-time, bidirectional AI communication system that turns a phone into an input device and a monitor into an AI interaction interface, enabling seamless two-way communication.
- **Solar Rooftop Design Platform**: Created a solar design platform that integrates CAD models with 3D simulations, adopted by multiple energy firms.
- **Cross-Platform VR Game**: Developed a VR game featuring audio, visual, and haptic feedback, reaching over 15,000 downloads.
- **Custom AR/VR Components**: Developed interactive 3D model elements using the WebXR SDK, enhancing user engagement and model interactivity.
